One blocker for the whole mess

Ads are just the start. Zenith stops the trackers, fingerprinting and nuisances that ride along with them — and lets you turn any of it on or off per site.

Ads

Banner, display, native and video ad requests, blocked at the network level before they load.

Trackers

Known analytics and tracking domains, plus learning that flags third parties seen across many sites.

Fingerprinting

Reduces canvas, WebGL and navigator fingerprinting, and sends Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.

Crypto-miners

Blocks known browser-based mining scripts so sites can't quietly use your CPU.

Cookie pop-ups

Automatically rejects or hides common consent banners, so you stop clicking "Accept".

Annoyances

Newsletter pop-ups, floating widgets, app banners and overlays, hidden out of your way.

Simple on the surface, detailed underneath

Everyday control lives in the toolbar. When you want the full story, the dashboard and the live activity view are one click away.

Control it from the toolbar

One click opens Zenith's popup: turn protection on or off, allow the site you're on, and see what's been blocked on this page — no settings to dig through.

  • Allow the current site in a single tap
  • A live count of what's blocked on this page
  • Six protections you can toggle any time

The whole picture on one dashboard

Open the dashboard for everything Zenith has blocked — the total, broken down by category, the domains behind it, and a live feed, all in one place.

  • Blocked requests broken down by category
  • Top blocked domains, ranked
  • Manage filter lists and allowed sites

See exactly what's blocked

Most blockers just show a number. Zenith's Blocked activity view shows the real requests it stops — the domain, the type, the moment it happened — live, as you browse. And it works in the published build, not just developer mode.

  • A live feed with type and search filters
  • Real URLs, not just a running count
  • Everything stays on your device — nothing uploaded

Choose exactly what to block

Zenith comes with EasyList, EasyPrivacy and Peter Lowe's list switched on — 11 curated lists in all, across ads, privacy, annoyances and security. Turn any of them on or off from the dashboard.

  • 11 lists, grouped by what they do
  • Sensible defaults on from the start
  • Add stronger lists when you want more blocking

Private by design, not by promise

Zenith has no account to create and no server to send anything to. Your settings, your allowlist and your block counts live only in your browser.

Filter lists are downloaded as plain text from trusted sources and treated as data — never run as code.
